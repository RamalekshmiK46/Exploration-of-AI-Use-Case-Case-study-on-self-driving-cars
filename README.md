# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
A self-driving car, also known as an autonomous vehicle, is a vehicle that uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to operate with little or no continuous human control. AI enables the vehicle to understand its surroundings, identify objects, make decisions, and safely travel from one location to another.

Self-driving cars use technologies such as Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, and Path Planning. Cameras, LiDAR, radar, GPS, and ultrasonic sensors collect information about the environment. Computer Vision and Deep Learning algorithms process this data to identify vehicles, pedestrians, traffic signs, traffic lights, road lanes, and obstacles.

The AI system then analyzes the collected information and determines the appropriate action, such as accelerating, braking, changing lanes, or turning. Path-planning algorithms help the vehicle select a safe route, while control systems convert the decisions into steering, braking, and acceleration commands.

The main AI functions in self-driving cars are:

Perception: Detects and identifies objects and road conditions.
Object Detection: Identifies vehicles, pedestrians, signs, and obstacles.
Decision Making: Determines what action the vehicle should take.
Path Planning: Selects a safe path to reach the destination.
Control: Controls steering, acceleration, and braking.
Machine Learning: Helps the system improve its ability to recognize and respond to different driving situations.

Thus, AI plays an important role in making self-driving cars safer, smarter, and more autonomous.

# RESULT
Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. It was understood that AI technologies such as Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, and Path Planning help autonomous vehicles perceive their surroundings, make intelligent decisions, and navigate safely with minimal human intervention.
